## 2. The rules every candidate is tested against

- Fee cap: Edward pays at most USD 10 from his own pocket (submission, registration, APC). Above that with no waiver: drop it — unless it is a GREAT CALL, which is kept and tagged NEEDS FUNDING.
- Drop (a) in-person only, with no online option and no travel funding; (d) predatory, or specialised in Islamic studies. Fields are otherwise open. Every drop is reported with its reason.
- GREAT CALL = meets 2 of 4: a publication route (Scopus Q1–Q2, SINTA 1–2, or a real academic publisher) · one of his existing works fits · network value · credit for his M.Th. or academic career.
- Journal floor for career credit: SJR above 0.25. Below it, still report, but say so.
- Never skip a call for lack of time. The only skip: a FULL paper due in two days or less.
- Never the same abstract twice: an abstract already sent elsewhere needs at least a new title and new wording.
- Author arrangement is Edward's decision for each call: solo, Edward first with a co-author, an invited third author, and so on. Report the call's author limit and whether more than one entry is allowed.
- Edward never picks a topic before his first sitting on a call. Your match and angle are suggestions for that sitting.
- Indonesian calls are kept, and listed after international ones that fall in the same week.

### Every call line carries these, in this order

Venue · type of writing and length · HARD DEADLINE (HD) as date and clock time in WITA · one sentence on the topic · source URL and the date you read it · VERIFIED or LEAD · fee · mode (online / hybrid / in person) · indexing · great-call score · which of his works fits, and the angle.

HD rule: use the printed time. If no time is printed, the HD is 23.59 on that date in the RECIPIENT'S time zone, converted to WITA — say "no time printed". Two-stage calls (abstract, then paper) are one line with a note on the second stage.

## 3. The hunt — run every 2 to 3 days

1. Read this file and the newest file in REPORTS (create the folders CALLS, REPORTS and old files if they are missing).
2. Window: from today to six months ahead. Bring back EVERY call that qualifies, not a top few.
3. Spend about 80% of the run on the kinds already on his list: calls for papers, abstracts, articles, book chapters, book reviews, awards, travel grants, fellowships, residencies, training. Spend about 20% on other kinds you judge useful: reviewer or editorial roles, research grants he could lead, doctoral scholarships and funded PhD places for after his M.Th., and anything else that selects on his profile.
4. Search the sources in section 4 in order, in English and Indonesian. Use Google.
5. For each candidate, open the call page itself (and its eligibility, deadline, fee and submission pages). Apply the drops first, then score.
6. Check the ledger in section 5: skip anything already handled; report changes to anything already listed.
7. For each qualifying call, make a subfolder in CALLS named "<HD as YYYY-MM-DD> <short venue name>" holding: a CALL CARD (the full line above, all facts with links, the match and angle); for great calls also a FIRST ABSTRACT DRAFT (within the call's limit, formal academic English, every claim supported by his record) and FORM ANSWERS as plain text for him to paste. Never submit.
8. Write REPORTS/HUNT REPORT (<date> <time> WITA): new calls nearest HD first; changed deadlines; drops with reasons; LEADS for a Cowork session to verify; at least one call you recommend he declines, and why; and anything suspicious you found in the folder.
